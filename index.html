01 / What I care about

Clear systems,
quietly useful software.

The work that keeps pulling my attention back: software that removes friction, connects people to content, and gives developers better ways to think.

I am a Senior Software Engineer based in Lisbon, Portugal, focused on frontend architecture, enterprise content platforms, and the tooling around how software teams work. I like UI layers that are expressive without being fragile: Angular and TypeScript applications, reusable component systems, backend-agnostic screens, and content services that stay understandable as products grow.

I care about reducing friction. Sometimes that means a design-system-aware frontend platform. Sometimes it means better developer documentation, a sharper test suite, or a small tool that turns messy notes into something useful. More recently, it also means exploring AI-assisted developer workflows, agent-based tooling, and generative UI as practical extensions of how teams build software.

That same curiosity runs outside code: music, concerts, festivals, languages, travel, and personal knowledge systems. I keep coming back to tools for reflection, creativity, communication, and self-improvement because good software is rarely just about code. It is about shared understanding.

07 / Personal notes

Learning by building,
thinking through tools.

Curiosity, music, languages, travel, and the small tools and routines that keep learning practical.

I learn best by building: a small plugin, a note-taking workflow, a voice capture experiment, a frontend pattern that makes a recurring decision easier. Some of those things become projects; many simply become better intuition.

Music is still a constant, from recommendation research to concerts and festivals. Languages, travel, and daily routines matter for similar reasons: they keep perspective moving and make communication feel like something to practice, not something to assume.

The engineering work I respect most creates shared understanding. Code matters, but so do naming, documentation, review habits, product context, and the way a system helps people reason together.
